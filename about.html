Our Story

Stone & Anvil began with a simple intention: create calm, lasting jewellery with thoughtful materials and hand-led craft.

Handmade Process

From sketch to final polish, each piece is formed by hand in small runs. We embrace slight variations as part of the character of true handmade work.

Sustainability

We prioritise recycled silver, mindful sourcing, and intentional production volumes to reduce waste while preserving quality.
